Microstrip Impedance Matching Transformer

  • Designed a three-section Chebyshev transformer to match 200 Ω to 50 Ω on Rogers RT/Duroid 5880
  • Simulation showed about 23 dB or better return loss from 2.5 to 5.5 GHz
  • Narrowest trace: 16.35 mil, above the 6 mil fabrication limit

Spring 2025

Neopixel Piano Teacher

  • Low-cost Raspberry Pi 4 add-on for a keyboard: NeoPixels show which key to press, GPIO switches detect presses, and a 7" touchscreen runs the menu
  • Python code loads MIDI songs and runs two modes: Teacher Mode waits for each correct key, Follow Mode plays in real time and checks every press is on beat
  • Prototype tests at 60, 90 and 120 BPM: near 100% accuracy at 60 and 90 BPM, which showed faster tempos needed better timing
  • User testing with 7 people: key error rate dropped from 0.22 to 0.11 and song time from about 30 s to 10 s over repeated tries
  • Worked on a 5-person team; contributed to hardware/software integration and user testing

Arduino Air Guitar

  • Glove with conductive fabric on the palm and fingers: touching a finger to the palm closes a circuit to an Arduino pin, and each finger picks a different note
  • Accelerometer in the strumming hand detects strums, and an ultrasonic sensor measures the distance between the hands to change pitch
  • Potentiometers set the note length and strum sensitivity
  • Sound made in C with Arduino timer interrupts and PWM, playing a stored guitar waveform to a speaker
  • Coordinated a 5-person team across hardware and software

Electronics 2

Optical Heartbeat Sensor

  • An IR LED shines through the finger and a phototransistor picks up the small change in light with each heartbeat (20 to 90 mV)
  • High-pass and low-pass RC filters keep the 1 to 5 Hz heartbeat and cut noise
  • Cascode MOSFET amplifier designed by hand for a gain of about 15 V/V, with bias and saturation checks
  • A MOSFET switch blinks an LED with each beat
  • The amplifier hit its 15 V/V target; the phototransistor also picked up heat from the finger, which threw off the reading and is the next thing to fix

Fall 2025 · Electronics 1

Non-Contact AC Voltage Detector

  • Pen-style tester that lights an LED near a live AC wire without touching it
  • A coil picks up a tiny current (about 1 µA) from the wire's electric field
  • Three cascaded 2N3904 NPN transistors boost it to about 5 mA to drive the LED, with each stage's operating region worked out by hand
  • Soldered on a protoboard and tested on a laptop charger: LED on when plugged in, off when unplugged
